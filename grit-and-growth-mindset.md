# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1
**Paraphrase the video in 1 or 2 lines.**

Grit means working hard towards our goals without giving up. Even when we face difficulties or failures, we should keep trying and continue working until we achieve our goals.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2
**Paraphrase the video in 1 or 2 lines.**

Growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and knowledge through hard work and practice. We should not be afraid of making mistakes because mistakes help us learn new things and become better.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions can change our results. The main point of the video is that we should take responsibility for our actions instead of blaming others or situations. We should focus on what we can control and try to improve ourselves.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4
**What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?**

1. Believe in yourself.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own learning plan.
4. Accept challenges and struggles.

## 5. Mindset – A MountBlue Warrior Reference Manual

### Question 5
**What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?**

As a software developer, I want to build a growth mindset by following these actions:

1. Practice coding every day to improve my programming skills.
2. Try to solve problems by myself before looking at the solutions.
3. Learn from my mistakes and understand why errors occur.
4. Accept feedback from mentors and work on improving myself.
5. Try to solve difficult problems instead of always choosing easy ones.
6. Learn new technologies and improve my knowledge step by step.
7. Manage my time properly and complete my daily learning tasks.
8. Never give up when I face difficulties in coding.
9. Ask questions whenever I don't understand something.
10. Focus on improving myself every day instead of comparing myself with others.

I believe that continuous learning, regular practice, and learning from mistakes will help me become a better software developer.
