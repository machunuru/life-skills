### Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you understand a concept by explaining it in simple words, as if you were teaching someone else.

### Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Focused Mode:** It is a state of thinking where we concentrate on solving a problem or understanding a new concept.
- **Diffused Mode:** It is a relaxed state of thinking where our brain connects different ideas, develops new thoughts, and helps us find solutions to problems.

### Question 4
**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. **Deconstruct the Skill:** Decide exactly what you want to learn and break it down into smaller parts.
2. **Learn Enough to Self-Correct:** Find three or four useful resources to understand the topic and identify your mistakes.
3. **Remove Practice Barriers:** Eliminate distractions such as mobile phones and television to improve concentration.
4. **Practice for at Least 20 Hours:** Spend enough time practising the topic to build confidence and improve your skills.
