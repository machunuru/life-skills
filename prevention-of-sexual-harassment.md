## Prevention of Sexual Harassment

**What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**
   
1. Touching someone without their permission.
2. Making unwanted sexual comments or jokes.
3. Asking someone for sexual favours.
4. Sending unwanted messages, photos or videos.
5. Making inappropriate gestures or staring at someone.
6. Asking someone for a date again and again after they say no.
7. Making someone feel uncomfortable or unsafe at work.
8. Threatening someone or promising benefits in return for sexual favours.
 **What would you do if you face or see any incident of such behaviour?**
1. I would tell the person to stop if I feel safe to do so.
2. I would note down the date, time and place of the incident.
3. I would save any messages or other proof.
4. I would report the incident to HR or the company's Internal Committee.
5. If I see someone facing harassment, I would support them.
6. I would encourage the affected person to report the incident.
7. I would cooperate with the investigation.
8. I would not ignore such behaviour, even if it happens repeatedly.
**I believe everyone should feel safe and respected in the workplace.**
