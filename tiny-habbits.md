## 2. Tiny Habits – Core Message

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P?

B = MAP is a formula that explains how to build a new habit.

- M – Motivation: It means having the interest to do something.
- A – Ability: It means how easy it is to do something.
- P – Prompt: It means a reminder that tells us to do something.

For example, if I want to read books every day, I can keep a book on my table. I can start by reading just one page every day. This makes reading easy and reminds me to do it.

### Question 3: Why is it important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of a habit?

For example, after completing my daily study goal, I can say to myself, "Good job!" This gives me a positive feeling and helps me continue the habit.

I learned that we should celebrate even small achievements because small successes help us build good habits.

## 4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits

### Question 5: What is the book's perspective about Identity?

According to the book *Atomic Habits*, we should focus on the person we want to become.

For example, instead of saying, "I want to learn programming," I can say, "I am a person who practises programming every day."

When we repeat good habits every day, we start believing in ourselves. Small actions help us build a good identity.

### Question 6: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

According to the book, we can make a habit easier by following these steps:

1. Make it obvious: Keep the things you need in a place where you can easily see them. For example, keep a book on your table.
2. Make it attractive: Choose something you enjoy doing.
3. Make it easy: Start with small tasks. For example, read just one page every day.
4. Two-Minute Rule: Start with an activity that takes only two minutes.

These steps help us start new habits without feeling lazy or stressed.

### Question 7: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

According to the book, we can stop bad habits by making them difficult.

1. Make it invisible: Keep things that cause bad habits away from you. For example, keep your phone away while studying.
2. Make it unattractive: Think about the bad effects of the habit.
3. Make it difficult:Make it harder to do the bad habit. For example, log out of social media accounts.
4. Make it unsatisfying: Track your bad habits and share your progress with someone.

These steps help us reduce bad habits and focus on good things.
